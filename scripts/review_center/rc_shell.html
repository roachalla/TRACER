How to review

  1. Left: the textbook exactly as students will get it, including the teaching materials (takeaways, study questions, glossary; untick Show teaching materials to see the plain edition).
  2. Right: one card for each thing that needs a person. Click a card to jump to its place in the text; click a numbered marker in the text to jump to its card.
  3. For each card choose Looks right, Needs a fix or Ask. For a sentence or equation, Needs a fix lets you type the corrected version; math goes between dollar signs, for example $\omega^2 = gk\tanh(kH)$.
  4. Comment on anything: select text in the textbook and press Comment.
  5. Your work is kept in this browser as you go. Press Save feedback to save it to a file: the first time, choose the shared OneDrive folder this page is in. After that, Save writes to the same file. Your designer receives it when OneDrive syncs.
  6. To continue on another computer, open this page there and press Open saved.
